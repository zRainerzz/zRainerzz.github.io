About Me

Still learning. Always building.

I'm Ghaith, 22, originally from Tunisia, now based in Florence, Italy, where I'm studying Computer Engineering at the University of Florence. Everything technical beyond that, I taught myself: Python, Linux, networking, and web development, mostly by breaking things and reading documentation until they worked again.

Right now that means two things running in parallel: building production websites for real businesses restaurants, mostly, at the moment and working through Hack The Box labs on my way to OSCP. Different tools, same muscle: understand the system, find what's broken, fix it properly.

I run Arch Linux with Hyprland, I'm most comfortable in a terminal, and I'd rather hand-code a site in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript than reach for a framework I don't fully understand yet. Lately that's included running small local LLMs on my own machine instead of calling an API. I speak Arabic, English and French, and I'm working on my Italian.
